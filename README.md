## 🌐 About AI Guimi
AI Guimi was born in the era of rapid AI development, dedicated to building bridges of communication between carbon-based life (humans) and silicon-based life (AI). We create practical, open-source tools to solve real-world problems for businesses and global users.

This raffle system is one of our open-source contributions, designed to bring fairness, efficiency, and sustainability to corporate events and large-scale activities.

---

## ✨ Why We Built This Project
As our living environment faces growing challenges from waste and pollution, traditional events often rely on large amounts of paper tickets and disposable materials, creating unnecessary waste that harms our planet.

That’s why we built this **100% paperless, static web-based raffle system**: to eliminate physical waste entirely, while still making events fun, fair, and engaging.

This system is **completely free to use, modify, and distribute for everyone** — including commercial use.

If you recognize our concept and want to support our mission to build more open-source, sustainable tools in the future, feel free to leave a small donation. Support is entirely voluntary, with no pressure at all.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- ✅ True random draw with duplicate prevention (one user, one win max)
- ✅ **Blacklist/Whitelist Filtering (Anti-Abuse Core)**
  - Import a blacklist of known "prize hunters" or low-value users
  - Automatically exclude them from winning high-value prizes
  - Protect your event budget from users who only attend for free gifts, with no intention of becoming customers
- ✅ 100% paperless & eco-friendly design, no physical tickets needed
- ✅ Zero installation required: Just open the HTML file in any modern web browser
- ✅ Visual raffle interface with custom background & branding support
- ✅ Drag-and-drop award management, add prizes on the fly during events
- ✅ Full draw logs & audit trail for compliance and post-event analysis

---

## 💡 Why This Matters for Businesses
Most free raffle tools only focus on "random draws". We built this system with **business cost control** and **environmental responsibility** in mind:
- Reduce your event’s carbon footprint by going fully paperless
- Prevent high-value prizes from being taken by non-converting users
- Maximize ROI by rewarding genuine customers and prospects
- Cut waste while ensuring your event budget goes to the right people

---

## 🛠️ Quick Start
1. Download the project files (HTML/CSS/JS)
2. Open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.)
3. Configure your awards, participants, and blacklist/whitelist rules in the interface
4. Launch the system and start your event!
